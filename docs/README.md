# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central index and quick guide to OctoAcme project management processes.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a lifecycle-based, iterative approach to project delivery. Teams begin by validating the problem, stakeholders, success measures, timeline, and initial risks during initiation. Approved work is then planned into prioritized, estimated increments with clear ownership, dependencies, milestones, acceptance criteria, and a Definition of Done. During execution, teams track work on a project board, collaborate through small pull requests, and review progress through standups, delivery syncs, and demos.

Communication and risk management continue throughout the lifecycle. Teams maintain a shared risk register, use a single source of truth for status, and escalate blockers from the team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor when appropriate. Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, post-deployment verification, and rollback planning. After releases, milestones, or incidents, retrospectives capture learnings and turn them into owned, measurable improvement actions.

Quality is built into the process through unit, integration, and end-to-end testing where appropriate; automated linting and security scanning; manual QA for feature acceptance; and release smoke tests. Clear roles support this workflow: Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work, Project Managers coordinate delivery and communication, Developers build and test solutions, QA validates quality, and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
