# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support delivery by managing engineering capacity, technical dependencies, team health, and execution risks. They connect engineering strategy with the project plan without replacing developers' ownership of implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Plan team capacity and surface resource constraints early
- Coordinate technical dependencies and sequencing with the Project Manager
- Support developers with prioritization, coaching, and delivery decisions
- Partner with QA/Testing to maintain quality and a sustainable delivery pace
- Escalate technical risks and staffing concerns through the agreed escalation path

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to understand priorities and trade-offs
- Work with the Project Manager to align capacity, milestones, dependencies, and risks
- Support Developers during estimation, design, implementation, and review
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on testability, quality risks, and readiness

---

## Design / UX Leads

### Role Summary
Design / UX Leads define the user experience direction and validate that proposed solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate discovery, user research, and usability validation where appropriate
- Produce or review user flows, interaction designs, and experience requirements
- Identify usability and accessibility risks before implementation
- Contribute user-centered acceptance criteria and design handoff materials
- Validate the delivered experience against intended outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to translate customer problems into product experiences
- Work with Developers to explain designs, resolve implementation questions, and balance usability with technical constraints
- Coordinate with Project Managers on design dependencies, review milestones, and delivery risks
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on usability, accessibility, and feature acceptance checks

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads help teams identify and manage security, privacy, regulatory, and control requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and compliance requirements during initiation and planning
- Identify threats, control gaps, data-handling concerns, and regulatory dependencies
- Recommend mitigations and track them in the risk register or project backlog
- Review security-sensitive designs, implementation plans, and release readiness
- Coordinate security incident or compliance escalation when required

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to record risks, owners, mitigations, and escalation paths
- Advise Product Managers on customer, legal, and business trade-offs
- Partner with Developers and Engineering Managers on secure design and implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security validation and with Stakeholders on required approvals

---

## Customer Success / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Leads represent customer experience and operational readiness concerns during delivery, helping the team turn real-world feedback into actionable improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, support trends, and known pain points
- Identify user impact, support risks, and documentation or training needs
- Review release communications and support readiness plans
- Help define escalation and feedback channels after launch
- Track recurring issues and connect them to backlog priorities

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to turn customer evidence into prioritization and success criteria
- Coordinate with Project Managers on stakeholder updates, readiness checkpoints, and release communications
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to reproduce issues and validate fixes
- Provide Stakeholders with customer-impact context during decisions and escalations

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers / Delivery Leads coordinate the transition from completed work to a safe, observable, and well-communicated release. This role may be performed by the Project Manager or another designated team member for smaller projects.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release sequencing, readiness reviews, and deployment windows
- Confirm acceptance criteria, CI results, security checks, smoke tests, and rollback plans
- Maintain release notes, stakeholder communications, and go/no-go decision records
- Coordinate post-deployment verification and follow-up actions
- Ensure incidents, rollback decisions, and lessons learned are captured

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to align release timing, dependencies, risks, and communications
- Coordinate with Product Managers on scope, business readiness, and stakeholder announcements
- Partner with Developers, Engineering Managers, and QA/Testing on technical readiness and verification
- Include Security / Compliance Leads, Customer Success / Support Leads, and Stakeholders in approvals appropriate to the release risk

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how project outcomes will be measured and help teams use reliable evidence to evaluate adoption, quality, and business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Translate project goals into measurable indicators and instrumentation needs
- Define measurement plans, data ownership, and reporting requirements
- Validate data quality and establish baseline or comparison measures
- Monitor success metrics after release and identify meaningful trends
- Communicate findings and recommend follow-up experiments or improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to define success metrics and interpret customer or business outcomes
- Coordinate with Project Managers to include measurement work, dependencies, and reporting milestones in the plan
- Partner with Developers and Engineering Managers on instrumentation, observability, and data quality
- Provide Stakeholders with evidence for go/no-go decisions, prioritization, and continuous improvement

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign a named owner for each role on a project, even when one person performs multiple roles.
- Use the interaction guidance to clarify handoffs, decision rights, dependencies, and escalation paths during initiation, planning, execution, and release.
